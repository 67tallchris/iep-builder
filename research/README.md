# IEP Builder — Research Packet (Phase 1)

**Status:** Draft for owner review (Phase 2 gate). Do not build from this yet.
**Scope:** Arizona K–8, mild/moderate disabilities — specific learning disability (SLD),
emotional disability (ED), other health impairment (OHI/ADHD), autism (mild),
speech-language impairment (SLI), and mild intellectual disability.
**Date:** Research session 1.

## What is in this packet

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `sources.md` | Annotated bibliography. Every claim in this research packet traces to one of these sources. |
| `az-process-guide.md` | The Arizona IEP lifecycle: referral → evaluation → MET eligibility → IEP → placement → annual review → reevaluation, with timelines, team rules, and Arizona-specific requirements. |
| `iep-components.md` | Component-by-component writing and quality guide (PLAAFP, goals, services, LRE, assessments, transition, ESY…), optimized for a future snippet library and compliance checker. |
| `behavior-guide.md` | Behavior as a special factor, FBA/BSP structure, crisis/safety planning, and Arizona restraint/seclusion rules. |
| `exemplars/` | 30 original, fictional, Arizona-aligned exemplar IEPs across SLD, ED, OHI/ADHD, mild autism, SLI, mild ID, 504 comparison, MTSS pre-referral, and behavior-support profiles. |
| `sources/` | The raw downloaded corpus (ADE documents, state exemplar PDFs, PBIS manuals, extracted text). |

## Review checklist for the owner (Phase 2)

Please mark up anything that does not match your program/placement experience:

1. **Accuracy** — Does the Arizona process description match what you see in schools?
2. **Terminology** — Do we use AZ terms correctly (MET, PWN, AzTAS, PLAAFP, ESY, LRE, SDI)?
3. **Exemplar mix** — Are the 30 profiles realistic for Marana-area elementary/middle classrooms? Add/drop/re-rank?
4. **Voice and length** — Is the PLAAFP/goal language at the right specificity and length?
5. **Categories** — Are there categories to add in v2 (hearing impairment, TBI, orthopedic, gifted/2e depth)?
6. **Gaps** — Anything missing that would make the tool unusable for you?

## Ground rules baked into this research

- **Synthetic exemplars only.** No real student names, records, or de-identified districts are used. All 30 exemplars are original, fictional composites modeled on public documents.
- **Not legal advice.** The tool drafts; the IEP team and public education agency remain responsible
  for legal compliance. Every export should carry this disclaimer.
- **No PII.** The tool must never be used with real student names or identifying data. A persistent
  banner is required on every page of the app.
- **Source bibliographies are public documents**, but some (notably vendor-published PDFs) are
  copyrighted. We cite and learn from structure/language; we do not republish them.
