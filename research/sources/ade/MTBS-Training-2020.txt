 Multi-Tier Behavior Supports Training for K-12


                 Multi-Tier Behavior Supports is a three-year training series that will assist
                 district, charter, or school teams with the development of a school-wide
                 approach for positive behavior management practices. This training is based
                 on School-Wide Behavior Intervention and Support (SW-PBIS) evidence-
                 based elements.
