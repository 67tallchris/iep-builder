# Arizona IEP Process Guide (K–8, mild/moderate)

**Primary source:** ADE/ESS, *AZ-TAS: Process for Developing Individualized Education Programs*,
November 2025 (`sources/ade/AzTAS-IEP-2025.pdf`), plus A.R.S. §§15-761, 15-763, 15-766, 15-767,
15-105. Citations below point to those sources.

> The tool drafts; the IEP team and public education agency (PEA) remain legally responsible.
> Nothing here is legal advice.

---

## 1. Process at a glance

```
Referral → Consent → Evaluation (60 days) → MET eligibility determination
   → IEP developed (within 30 days of eligibility) → Placement/LRE → Services begin
   → Annual review each year → Reevaluation at least every 3 years
```

Arizona uses **MET** (Multidisciplinary Evaluation Team) for evaluation/eligibility and the
**IEP team** for program development. Both include parents and required school members; the same
person may fill multiple roles (A.R.S. §15-761; AzTAS 2025 pp. 23–25).

## 2. Referral and consent

- A special education referral is made under the direction of the district/charter chief
  administrator or designee, after consultation with the parent (A.R.S. §15-766(A)).
- The PEA must explicitly notify the parent that they may **consent to or refuse** the initial
  evaluation (A.R.S. §15-766(B)).
- Evaluation must be conducted **within 60 days after receiving parental consent**
  (A.R.S. §15-766(B)).

## 3. Evaluation and eligibility (MET)

- Evaluation must be sufficiently comprehensive to identify all suspected disabilities and
  service needs — not just the referral question.
- Arizona categories relevant to this tool: specific learning disability (SLD), emotional
  disability (ED), other health impairment (OHI), autism, speech/language impairment (SLI),
  mild intellectual disability (ID), and developmental delay for eligible young children
  (A.R.S. §15-761).
- The MET determines eligibility. Categories may not be based on lack of appropriate
  instruction in reading/math or on English-language difference (A.R.S. §15-761(B)).
- **SLD + dyslexia:** if the student is found eligible under SLD, the IEP must indicate whether
  the student has been **diagnosed with dyslexia** (A.R.S. §15-763). Make this a required field
  for SLD profiles in the tool.
- Reevaluation: not more than once a year (unless parent and PEA agree) and **at least every
  three years**, unless waived by agreement; earlier if needs warrant or parent/teacher requests
  (A.R.S. §15-766(B)).

## 4. IEP development and required components

- The IEP must be developed **within 30 days of the eligibility determination** and be in effect
  at the beginning of each school year; initial services begin as soon as possible after
  development (AzTAS 2025 p. 3).
- Required components (AzTAS 2025 pp. 4–23; 34 C.F.R. §300.320), summarized:
  1. Present levels of academic achievement and functional performance (PLAAFP)
  2. Measurable annual goals
  3. Benchmarks/short-term objectives **required** only for students on the alternate assessment
  4. Progress reporting (how and when)
  5. Special education services, related services, supplementary aids and services
     (frequency, duration, type, location, provider)
  6. Program modifications and supports for school personnel
  7. Explanation of the extent of non-participation with nondisabled peers (LRE statement)
  8. Assessment accommodations for state/PEA-wide assessments
  9. Transition (beginning with the first IEP in effect when the student completes 9th grade or
     reaches age 16, whichever is first; updated annually)
  10. Transfer of rights statement beginning one year before age 18
- **Arizona adds:** incorporation of Arizona Academic Standards, grade-level expectations and
  content; for SLD, the dyslexia statement; ESY consideration; AzEIP transition considerations.

## 5. IEP team membership and meeting rules

Required team members (AzTAS 2025 pp. 23–24):
- Parent(s)
- At least one general education teacher (if the student is/may be in general education)
- At least one special education teacher/provider
- A PEA representative who is qualified to provide/supervise SDI, knows the general curriculum,
  and can commit PEA resources (may double as another role)
- Someone who can interpret instructional implications of evaluation results
- Others at parent/PEA discretion (including related services providers)
- The student, whenever appropriate (must be invited if transition is discussed)
- Outside agency representatives with consent (transition)
- AzEIP coordinator for initial IEP for children transitioning from AzEIP (at parent request)
- Private school representative if applicable

Meeting rules:
- Provide **early written notice** at a mutually agreed time/place; document parent attempts if
  the parent cannot attend (AzTAS 2025 pp. 26–27).
- Requirement to ensure parent understanding; interpreter arranged when needed.
- **Excusal:** a required member may be excused, in whole or in part, with written parent and PEA
  consent, and that member must submit written input in advance (AzTAS 2025 p. 25).
- **Consensus, not majority vote.** If the team cannot reach consensus, the PEA representative
  makes the final decision and must explain the proposal/refusal in a **Prior Written Notice
  (PWN)** to parents (AzTAS 2025 p. 25).
- Meetings may be held without the parent if attendance cannot be arranged, with detailed records
  of attempts; alternative participation (video/conference call) is allowed (AzTAS 2025
  pp. 26–27).
- After an annual review, the parent and PEA may agree to amend the IEP in writing without a
  meeting; the whole team must be informed, and a PWN + revised IEP provided (AzTAS 2025 p. 27).

## 6. Review, amendment, and reevaluation

- Annual review at least once a year, and more often if progress substantially deviates from
  expectations (AzTAS 2025 p. 23).
- A parent or PEA may request a review in writing with a stated reason; it must be convened
  within **45 school days** of receipt (AzTAS 2025 p. 23).
- Review all sections and revise as appropriate.
- Reevaluation as described in §3 above.

## 7. LRE and placement

- Students must be educated with nondisabled peers to the maximum extent appropriate; removal
  only when education in regular classes with supplementary aids/services cannot be achieved
  satisfactorily (AzTAS 2025 pp. 13–14, p. 23).
- Placement is determined **after** PLAAFP/goals/services, starts from the general education
  classroom, and must consider the continuum of alternative placements and potential harmful
  effects. More restrictive placement must not reduce access to the general curriculum.
- If the student will not participate with nondisabled peers for any part of the day, the IEP
  must give a **detailed, specific explanation**; if fully included, stating that is sufficient.

## 8. Extended school year (ESY)

- ESY is available as necessary for FAPE, at no cost to parents; eligibility is individual and
  cannot be limited by category or by unilateral limits on type/amount/duration (AzTAS 2025
  p. 22; A.A.C. R7-2-408).
- Two prongs: (1) benefits would be significantly jeopardized without services, or (2) severe/
  substantial regression with serious impediment to progress.
- **Determine ESY eligibility no later than 45 days before the last day of the school year**,
  using retrospective (regression/recoupment) and predictive data (AzTAS 2025 p. 22).
- Document the annual consideration for all students.

## 9. Transition (secondary, for completeness)

- Begins with the first IEP in effect when the student completes 9th grade or reaches age 16,
  whichever is first (or younger if the team decides); updated annually (AzTAS 2025 p. 15).
- Measurable postsecondary goals based on age-appropriate transition assessments: training/
  education and employment required; independent living if appropriate.
- Transition services/activities (instruction, related services, community experiences,
  employment, adult living, daily living), courses of study (specific courses, not "elective"),
  and annual goals that support the postsecondary goals.
- Include the anticipated graduation date and notify parents in writing at least one year before
  anticipated graduation.
- Invite the student and (with consent) outside agencies.

## 10. Transfer of rights, transfers between schools

- Beginning not less than one year before age 18, include a statement that the student has been
  informed of rights that transfer at age 18 (AzTAS 2025 pp. 19–20).
- In-state transfer: receiving school provides FAPE with comparable services until it adopts the
  prior IEP or develops a new one; records requested within 10 school days and sent within
  10 school days for in-state PEAs (AzTAS 2025 pp. 27–28).
- Out-of-state transfer: comparable services until Arizona evaluation/eligibility (if needed) and
  a new IEP.

## 11. Prior Written Notice (PWN) and dispute options

- PWN is required for proposals/refusals to initiate or change identification, evaluation,
  placement, or FAPE provision; it documents the decision, basis, options considered, and sources
  used. (ADE's 2023 *IEP scenario* training document turns on this question.)
- Parents may request mediation or file a due process complaint. Arizona also offers the
  **Facilitated IEP (FIEP)** process as a voluntary, early dispute-prevention option.

## 13. Arizona-specific compliance callouts for the tool

1. **Dyslexia statement required on every SLD IEP** (A.R.S. §15-763).
2. **Arizona Academic Standards language** must be incorporated into PLAAFP and goals
   (AzTAS 2025 pp. 4–5).
3. **Benchmarks/STOs only required for alternate-assessment students.**
4. **State assessments:** AASA, AzELLA, AZSci; alternate assessments MSAA/Alt-ELPA only when
   eligibility criteria on the Alternate Assessment Eligibility form are met; third-grade
   **Move On When Reading (MOWR)** requirements must be considered.
5. **PWN + parent copy of IEP** at no cost; best practice is a draft IEP before the meeting.
6. **Arizona uses "emotional disability" (ED)**, not "emotional disturbance."
7. **Mild intellectual disability** is its own Arizona category distinct from moderate/severe.
8. **Facilitated IEP (FIEP)** is an Arizona-specific early dispute option.
